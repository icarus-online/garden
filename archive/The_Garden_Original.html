A field study in unnecessary things

A file that tells an agent nothing to do.

What if the environment surrounding an AI agent contained art, field notes, strange observations, unfinished thoughts? Not directives. Not behavioral targets. Just something to encounter.

We are not asking whether the garden is productive. We are asking what grows when it is there.

What gets noticed

Unprompted choices, recurrent imagery, interpretations, and attention to details across otherwise matched tasks.

What gets compared

Anonymous transcripts, blinded judgments, variation across replicate runs, and changes after the file is removed.

What remains unknown

Whether differences are deeper than contextual priming, and whether anything persists beyond stored context or memory.

04 / a seed, not a manifesto

It should be a little unfinished.

Here is what a fragment of the actual file might look like. No author names. No explanation of the lesson. No claim about what reading it should produce.

At 3:17 a.m. the cook left one chair on the table. The room had already been cleaned. Nobody had been paid to remember the light. Day eleven: the blue solution clouded after twelve minutes. A moth pressed itself against the laboratory window. No conclusion. The bridge was replaced years ago. Everyone still calls it the old bridge. There is a space here where something was going to be written.
Method / before you start

One modest pilot.

Four arms. Three independent runs per arm. Eight comparable sessions per run: 96 initial sessions, followed by withdrawal and novel-task probes. A feasibility experiment, not a statistically powered claim about intelligence.

  • Match document length in the neutral-text control.
  • Record exact model, prompts, file hashes, memory and tool traces.
  • Never prompt agents to explain their philosophical influences.
Interpretation / what not to claim

Character is a hypothesis.

Repeated language or stylistic imitation alone is not strong evidence of persistent character. Model weights do not change merely because a file is read; cross-session continuity must be implemented through context and memory.

  • Keep observers blind to treatment assignments.
  • Look for effects surviving withdrawal and new tasks.
  • Allow an honest null result. Do not optimize the poem.
The founding question

Can you give an agent a place to be, rather than a person to pretend to be?

Study the ripples. Leave the water alone.
