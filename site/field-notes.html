22 October 2026Method / 003

When the file leaves,
what has actually left?

Removing Philosophy.md does not necessarily remove its language. An enabled memory system may retain quotations, paraphrases, or associations from earlier responses. “Withdrawal” therefore names the removal of the original file, not proof that all traces of its content are gone.

The study must inspect the mechanism of persistence. A remembered passage and a broad behavioral difference are not interchangeable observations.

22 October 2026Design / 002

The empty laboratory.

A working research interface is not a completed study. The public laboratory offers conditions, allocation planning, and an honest view of missing traces. It does not manufacture interesting replies to fill the silence.

Before the pilot: model locking, control matching, task freezing, isolation validation, reviewer preparation, and a documented execution decision.
