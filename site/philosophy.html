The artistic commitment

Leave room
for the gratuitous.

A poem does not have to earn its place by moving a metric. We may improve how we observe the garden. We do not rewrite the garden to make it perform better.

The file is not a system prompt, a constitution, or an evaluation rubric. In the harness it remains low-trust content, with no authority over tools or permissions.
