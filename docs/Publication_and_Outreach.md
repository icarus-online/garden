# COMMUNICATING THE GARDEN / INVITING SKEPTICS
Icarus Online LLC, independent research; publication not yet made.

## A possible short LinkedIn research note

I built a file that doesn't tell my AI agents what to do.

Actually, that's getting ahead of myself: I've built the file and a research design. I don't yet know whether it works. I'm not even sure what "works" would mean here.

It's called The Garden. Think field notes from a kitchen, an unfinished laboratory observation, a fragment of weather. It has no values checklist, goals, or productivity metric. It exists because I wanted to see what happens when an agent lives alongside non-prescriptive cultural material.

Under Icarus Online LLC, I'm designing a randomized experiment: same model, same tasks, Garden versus matched neutral context, each with and without controlled external memory. Then remove the file, restart the sessions, and see whether anything detectable survives in novel tasks. We will look for ordinary prompt effects, memory leakage, and the possibility of no effect at all.

I would genuinely like to hear from people who think this is misguided, anthropomorphic, or methodologically flawed. What controls would convince you that any difference is real, rather than a poetic illusion?

No claims yet. Just a garden and a laboratory beside it.

## What not to claim in early outreach

- Don't say agents developed souls, minds, consciousness, or inner lives.
- Don't say the intervention worked until a blind confirmatory analysis supports a narrowly defined result.
- Don't say original/first-ever before a thorough systematic literature review.
- Don't attribute fictional AI opinions to real philosophical thinkers.
- Don't imply Soberlink participation, ownership, or employer sponsorship.
- Don't publish unlicensed source excerpts, private recording, personal data, or employer code.

## Minimum public package before announcing 'experiments are underway'

- Frozen corpus hashes and rights.
- Authorized clean-room test harness and memory separation tests.
- Task bank, tokenizer-based text-length controls.
- Locked pilot plan and data ethics audit.
- Honest status statement: design, feasibility pilot, or preregistered confirmation.

Link to this website as a concept and design, not as evidence of experimental results.
